For parents

Is your child’s e-bike legal?

Illegal e-bikes can be seized and destroyed, and a serious accident can change a family’s life. Check before you buy or let your child ride.

A legal e-bike has

  • Pedals, and a motor that assists while you pedal
  • A motor of 250W or less (NSW allows 500W until 28 Feb 2029)
  • Assistance that cuts out at 25 km/h
  • A throttle only up to walking pace (6 km/h)

An illegal e-bike is

  • Over the power or speed limits, or modified or “unlocked”
  • Treated as an unregistered motor vehicle
  • Liable to be seized and destroyed in NSW and Queensland
  • A fine for the rider

Age rules vary by state

Western Australia: minimum age 16. Queensland: 16 with a licence, or 12 to 17 with supervision. Other states have no general minimum age for a legal e-bike (NSW is considering one). Check your state.

Is your house at risk?

If an underage rider seriously injures someone on an illegal e-bike, care costs can run into millions, and the injured person’s lawyers can look to the parents, especially if they bought the bike or let the child ride it. Home insurance may not cover it.
