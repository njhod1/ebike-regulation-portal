\section*{1. THE COMPLIANCE RULE \& KEY DATES}
Under the \textbf{Road Rules 2014}, any e-bike used in public must be an EPAC (Electrically Power Assisted Cycle).
\begin{itemize}[label=--, nosep, topsep=3pt, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{1 Feb 2026:} All new e-bikes sold in NSW must carry a Fair Trading \textbf{Certificate of Approval}.
    \item \textbf{1 Mar 2026:} Power limit dropped 500\,W $\rightarrow$ \textbf{250\,W}. Grace period for existing 500\,W owners until \textbf{1 Mar 2029}.
    \item \textbf{24 Mar 2026:} \textbf{Road Transport Amendment Bill 2026} enacted --- Police can seize and crush illegal e-bikes.
    \item \textbf{1 Mar 2029:} Grace period ends. All e-bikes must comply with \textbf{EN 15194}. No exceptions.
    \item \textbf{Rating $>$ 250W (new):} Legally a \textbf{Motorcycle}. Riding without registration/insurance/licence is a criminal offence.
\end{itemize}

\section*{2. SEIZURE \& RETAILER ACCOUNTABILITY}
Pursuant to the \textbf{Road Transport Amendment (Non-registrable Motor Vehicles) Act 2026}:
\begin{itemize}[label=--, nosep, topsep=3pt, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Seizure \& Crush:} Non-compliant bikes can be seized and \textbf{destroyed} with no return option.
    \item \textbf{Retailer Warning:} Selling motorbikes as ``legal e-bikes'' breaches \textbf{Australian Consumer Law}. Report to Fair Trading.
    \item \textbf{Dyno Testing:} NSW Police use portable roadside Dyno units to verify motor cuts out at 25\,km/h.
\end{itemize}

\section*{3. FEDERAL IMPORT \& OFF-ROAD USE}
The \textbf{Road Vehicle Standards Act 2018} restricts high-powered e-motorbikes.
\begin{itemize}[label=--, nosep, topsep=3pt, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Off-Road Only:} Motors over 250\,W are strictly for \textbf{Private Property} and cannot be registered for road use.
    \item \textbf{Federal Import Ban (Dec 2025):} The Federal Government banned import of e-bikes not meeting EN 15194.
    \item \textbf{No Loophole:} All public road use of $>$250\,W bikes ceases 1 Mar 2029 regardless of purchase date.
\end{itemize}
